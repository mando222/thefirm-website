Criminal Defense

Facing criminal charges can be one of the most stressful and uncertain experiences in life. Whether you are accused of a misdemeanor or a serious felony, you deserve a defense attorney who will listen to your story, protect your rights, and fight for your future.

We provide experienced criminal defense representation for clients charged with offenses such as DUI, drug crimes, assault, theft, domestic violence, and other state-level charges. From the moment we take your case, we work to ensure that your rights are protected and that you fully understand your options.

We carefully review the evidence, challenge improper procedures, and build strategic defenses tailored to your specific circumstances. Our approach is straightforward and client-focused: we tell you what to expect, explain every step, and fight hard for the best possible outcome.

Everyone deserves fair treatment and a strong advocate. Your constitutional rights are guaranteed by both the United States and Tennessee Constitutions, and our belief is that those rights should never be violated or disregarded.
