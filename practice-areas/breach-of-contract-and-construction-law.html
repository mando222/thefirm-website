Breach of Contract & Construction Law

Contracts are the foundation of business and property relationships. When one party fails to uphold their obligations, legal action may become necessary. We represent individuals, contractors, and business owners in disputes involving breach of contract, nonpayment, defective work, construction delays, and other commercial disagreements.

We focus on resolving disputes efficiently, through negotiation or litigation as appropriate. We also assist clients with drafting, reviewing, and enforcing contracts to help prevent future conflicts.

In construction matters, we understand the pressures and complexities involved, including tight timelines, subcontractor coordination, and budget constraints. Our goal is to provide practical, effective legal solutions that protect your financial interests and business relationships.

Whether you are enforcing a contract or defending against a claim, we offer thorough preparation, honest advice, and determined advocacy to help achieve a fair outcome. Please note that we do not handle arbitration matters nor prime or remote contractor's liens upon real property.
